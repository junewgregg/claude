## Focus areas
- Therapeutic areas / modalities we prioritize (e.g. immuno-oncology, targeted

- Mechanisms or platforms we explicitly avoid.

## Stage and risk
- Preferred clinical stage at entry (e.g. de-risked Phase 2 with proof-of-concept).
- Tolerance for binary clinical risk and platform risk.

## Commercial bar
- Minimum addressable market / peak-sales expectation.
- Pricing power, reimbursement, and competitive-intensity preferences.

## IP and exclusivity
- Required exclusivity runway post-investment.

## Deal structure
- Check size, ownership, and structure (equity, royalty, structured) that fits
  our mandate.

## What "strong fit" looks like
- A short description of the archetype asset we would compete hard to win.
