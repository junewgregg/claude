| Evidence review |
Class rationale: targeting MAP as a root-cause hypothesis is differentiated in Crohn's and could complement existing immune modulation.
Differentiation claim: oral administration and non-overlapping toxicity versus biologics are attractive if efficacy is maintained.
Lead-indication evidence: sponsor cites the randomized Phase 3 MAP US study of predecessor RHB-104 meeting remission and secondary endpoints, including endoscopic improvement in a smaller colonoscopy subset.
Combination logic: positive safety with concomitant corticosteroids, immunomodulators and anti-TNF therapy supports future combination or sequencing flexibility.
Expansion-indication evidence: focus should stay on MAP-positive Crohn's until that thesis is clinically locked.
