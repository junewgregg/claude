| Executive conclusion |
Lead asset framing: First potent/selective allosteric EGFR inhibitor designed for post-osimertinib resistance mutations where current EGFR options remain incomplete.
Why it lands at Diligence: The precision-oncology fit is excellent and the early selectivity profile is promising. The constraints are preclinical stage, niche market size, pending GLP tox, and formulation/epimerization issues that still need to be de-risked.
Best-fit entry indication and patient population: EGFR LR-positive NSCLC patients acquiring C797S-containing resistance after osimertinib, especially where WT selectivity could widen the therapeutic window.
Commercially attractive but not first-line screening focus: Could become a valuable bolt-on precision oncology asset if a clean development path and differentiated tolerability are confirmed.

| Evidence review |
Class rationale: C797S-mediated resistance after osimertinib remains a real unmet need in EGFR-mutant NSCLC.
Differentiation claim: sponsor describes superior wild-type selectivity versus earlier EGFR generations and regression in preclinical models.
Lead-indication evidence: preclinical tumor regressions and a favorable early rat minitox NOAEL are supportive, but no human data exist yet.
Combination logic: future combination with anti-EGFR antibodies or other TKIs could emerge, but the first job is to get a clean monotherapy path into the clinic.
Expansion-indication evidence: market remains niche; value depends on selecting the exact resistance subset well.
