| Evidence review |
Class rationale: PI3K/AKT/mTOR signaling is validated in HR+ breast cancer and prostate cancer, especially after endocrine or AR-pathway resistance.
Differentiation claim: sponsor argues efficacy/safety comparable to capivasertib with convenience as a potential differentiator.
Lead-indication evidence: FINER reportedly showed statistically significant and clinically meaningful PFS benefit in ITT and a stronger effect in PIK3CA/AKT/PTEN-altered HR+ mBC.
Combination logic: endocrine combination in HR+ disease is already built into the lead story; future biomarker-selected combinations are optional, not required for core value.
Expansion-indication evidence: NGS-defined benefit in mCRPC strengthens the platform thesis without needing to dilute the breast first path.
