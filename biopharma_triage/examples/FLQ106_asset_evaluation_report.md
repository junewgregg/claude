| Evidence review |
Class rationale: broad inhibition of RGD-binding integrins is coherent for fibrovascular tissue growth control in pterygium, but the package is primarily sponsor-framed and not yet decision-grade for differentiation.
Differentiation claim: current SoC is observation and surgery; a non-surgical medical option would be differentiated if efficacy and tolerability translate clinically.
Lead-indication evidence: the uploaded deck shows pterygium market framing and integrin potency data, but little asset-specific human efficacy detail for FLQ-106 in the current package.
Combination logic: limited current relevance; the cleaner first path is stand-alone disease modification in progressive corneal disease.
Expansion-indication evidence: broader FELIQS retina data support sponsor credibility but do not directly de-risk FLQ-106.
