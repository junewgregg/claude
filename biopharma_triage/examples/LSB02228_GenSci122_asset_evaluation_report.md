Executive conclusion
Lead asset framing: GenSci122 is a potential best-in-class oral KIF18A inhibitor for chromosomally unstable tumors, especially high-grade serous ovarian cancer and related solid tumors.
Why it lands at Diligence: The asset fits the screen well because it addresses an on-priority ovarian/solid-tumor biology with a preferred small-molecule modality and rides a class that now has first clinical proof from Volastra’s VLS-1488. It remains at Diligence because GenSci-specific evidence is still preclinical/very early clinical.
Best-fit entry indication and patient population: Platinum-resistant HGSOC with high chromosomal instability, where the first external clinical proof-of-concept for KIF18A has already appeared.
Commercially attractive but not first-line screening focus: CIN-high TNBC and broader solid tumors are logical later expansions once ovarian dosing and response benchmarks are clearer.

Evidence review
Class rationale: KIF18A is a compelling synthetic-lethal target in CIN tumors because normal cells are relatively less dependent on it for division.
Differentiation claim: GenSci argues best-in-class potential through better cellular potency and lower efficacious exposure than AMG650 and VLS-1488 in preclinical models.
Lead-indication evidence: The sponsor shows stronger efficacy at lower exposure in OVCAR3/HCC15 models, while external 2025 Volastra data showed tumor reduction in 7 of 17 heavily pretreated ovarian patients and favorable safety—important class validation but not yet GenSci validation.
Combination logic: Combination is plausible but should follow monotherapy or clean targeted proof in HGSOC first.
Expansion-indication evidence: CIN is common across solid tumors, but ovarian remains the best near-term proof point.
