| Executive conclusion |
Lead asset framing: Clinically exposed redoseable iNKT agonist intended to re-ignite innate and adaptive immunity upstream of PD-1, with a parallel metabolic thesis in obesity.
Why it lands at Diligence: The human exposure, tolerability and mechanistic differentiation are real strengths. The main gating issues are breadth of ambition, reliance on sponsor-curated early activity, and the need to tighten the first oncology path around CD1d-rich tumors.
Best-fit entry indication and patient population: Checkpoint-exposed patients in CD1d-rich tumors where innate immune suppression is prominent and a biomarker-driven Phase Ib can read out quickly.
Commercially attractive but not first-line screening focus: Obesity/MASH adds optionality, but it should not be allowed to distract from proving the oncology thesis first.

| Evidence review |
Class rationale: iNKT agonism is upstream of PD-1 and can reprogram macrophages, NK/CD8 cells and cytokine signaling, giving a coherent rescue-of-resistance rationale.
Differentiation claim: sponsor emphasizes repeat dosing, favorable safety and 50-100x iNKT expansion versus prior agonist limitations.
Lead-indication evidence: 15 oncology patients were reportedly redosed with favorable safety and signs of immune/clinical activity, but the disclosed efficacy dataset remains summarized rather than exhaustive.
Combination logic: checkpoint combinations are central and appear mechanistically natural.
Expansion-indication evidence: obesity/MASH may create financing flexibility but also strategic sprawl.
