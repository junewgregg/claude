Evidence review
Class rationale: Synthetic lethality between BET inhibition and EP300 LOF is biologically intuitive and supported by in vivo models, variant mapping, and real-world-data-backed platform work.
Differentiation claim: The core differentiation is not a novel target class but a retargeted, clinically de-risked molecule applied to an under-served tumor-suppressor-loss biology where there are no approved targeted options.
Lead-indication evidence: Two earlier Phase I studies in 87 patients established favorable safety/tolerability and dose-dependent BET inhibition, while Leapfrog shows EP300-driven NSCLC and PDX sensitivity at clinically achievable doses and an active IND with rapid trial-readiness.
Combination logic: Combination optionality exists, but the first win should come from monotherapy or tightly controlled targeted development in EP300 LOF disease before adding complexity.
Expansion-indication evidence: Cross-tumor EP300 LOF prevalence and variant-enrichment work support a broader precision-oncology franchise after the first cohort de-risks the biology.
