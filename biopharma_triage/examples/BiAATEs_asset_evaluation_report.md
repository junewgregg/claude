Screening view: Highly novel, mechanistically targeted autoimmune concept with a tight first indication, but the actual stage is much earlier than the batch table suggests and that keeps the rating below Acquire.
| Executive conclusion |
Lead asset framing: First-in-class BiAATE platform designed to selectively deplete PLA2R-directed autoreactive B cells in membranous nephropathy while avoiding broad B-cell depletion.
Why it lands at Diligence: The concept is elegant and the first indication is biologically specific, but current proof is preclinical and the immediate plan is still non-GLP tox rather than clinical execution.
Best-fit entry indication and patient population: PLA2R-positive primary membranous nephropathy patients at high renal-risk who would benefit from targeted autoimmune depletion without blanket immunosuppression.
Commercially attractive but not first-line screening focus: If the targeting paradigm works, platform extension into other autoantibody-driven diseases could materially expand value.

| Evidence review |
Class rationale: selective depletion of disease-causing autoreactive B cells is more precise than current broad immunosuppression or pan-B-cell depletion.
Differentiation claim: the platform seeks to spare the broader B-cell lineage by redirecting T-cell killing only toward autoreactive BCR-bearing cells.
Lead-indication evidence: sponsor shows transgenic-mouse reduction of disease-causing antibodies and cites mechanistic publication support, but no human data exist yet.
Combination logic: first path should remain monotherapy in a biomarker-defined nephrology subset rather than early combination complexity.
Expansion-indication evidence: platform promise is real but must remain secondary to proving the first construct in PMN.
