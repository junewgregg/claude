| Evidence review |
Class rationale: targeted membrane disruption with immunogenic cell death is mechanistically differentiated and fits difficult liver-dominant disease.
Differentiation claim: liver persistence and a CLIA assay with reported 100% predictive value are the strongest points in the package.
Lead-indication evidence: sponsor highlights 69% ORR in a Phase 2a post-hoc analysis among ovarian cancer patients with liver metastases, but direct HCC validation remains pending.
Combination logic: could pair well with checkpoint or anti-angiogenic regimens in HCC, but that should be second step after monotherapy/selected-population proof.
Expansion-indication evidence: liver-metastatic cancers are a real optionality bucket because the PK story is liver-centric.

| Regulatory path, commercial framing, and thesis test |
Execution status: A focused HCC validation and dose-optimization plan is coherent and the requested Series A fits the screen better than a broad registrational ambition today.

What broadening too early would risk: Leaning into many metastatic tumor types before HCC proof would blur signal interpretation and capital use.
