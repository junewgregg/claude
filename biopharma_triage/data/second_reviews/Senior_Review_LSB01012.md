Detailed review of LSB-01-012 (SAR113945) 
 
CMC:  I can follow the arguments for and agree with the scoring (a few critical notes 
were flagged to be followed up). 
 
Regulatory:  I can agree with the scoring. 
 
Clinical: I would like to discuss / dig deeper on the scoring of the clinical study 
logistics. The paper describing the early clinical results refers to: "the screening 
procedure of such a trial may also necessitate other imaging techniques such as 
sonography and semi-quantitative magnetic resonance imaging, techniques." Is this 
commonly done and good to quantify? Could this lead to a lower scoring? 
Could this drug also be positioned in RA that is confined to larger joints? 
 
Technical: I like to discuss the scoring as systemic tox concerns were flagged and I 
want to understand better if the WOMAC score used in the early clinical studies as well 
as the animal models included in the IB capture the drugs MoA. Are the systemic drug 
levels seen in the clinical studies in the drugs IC 50 range? Could the drug be 
formulated in such a way that it is only taken up by relevant target cells in the joint? 
 
Commercial: I think the score is too high. The drug has already been demonstrated 
NOT to work in the general disease population. It may work in pre-selected patients 
with high inflammatory score (don't know how accepted (and costly) it is to select those 
patients and what % of the over all number of OA patients it represents. Potential for 
premium drug pricing seems not possible.  
 
Overall second oppinion review: We need to dig a bit deeper on the potential side 
effects of systemic exposure and what the variability is of the PK profile. We should also 
dig a bit deeper if drug can be formulated to target specific cell and if RA (and other AIDs 
could be an option if it could be target to soecific cells. Also we should think about 
positioning / testing this drug in OA in dogs (which is if fact a significant market by 
itself).
