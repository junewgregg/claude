Detailed review of LSB-01-006 (IPN60210​)
CMC: I support the CMC scoring given it is oral small molecule epigenetic inhibitor with a relatively conventional CMC development pathway.
Regulatory: I agree with the scoring. Multiple myeloma (MM) can be and has been eligible for orphan drug designation in the United States under the FDA’s Orphan Drug Act.  As a small molecule, phase 2 and 3 will be needed but accelerated development timelines are possible. Clinical data depending – could be eligible for Breakthrough designation
Clinical: I agree with the clinical assessment.  IPN60210 is a small molecule noncompetitive inhibitor of SETD2, a methyltransferase that catalyzes trimethylation of histone H3. IPN60210 was demonstrated to be highly active in diffuse large B-Cell lymphoma (DLBCL) and multiple myeloma (MM) cell lines.  The asset is supported by dose predictions, and clinical dose escalation experience in 1b trials.  The unmet need clinically is precluded by multiple approved therapeutics in MM and DLBCL.
Technical: I recommend raising the scoring for both technical assets based upon a collective comprehension of the technical and clinical reviews.  The technical review should be at 3 - Cohort case study epidemiologic data supports an association and 3 - Comprehensive preclinical package complete and demonstrates safety and efficacy.  This is based upon the in-clinic progress and the available pre-clinical data that is extensive, incl., large panels of MM And DLBCL cell lines, synergistic efficacy in vitro with MM SOC, PK in mouse, & safety studies in dogs.
Commercial: I agree with the commercial scoring for all categories given the highly competitive nature of the categories targeted; but also recognizing the need for additional therapies that have activity in refractory patients.
Collective Review by PWT:
Overall, this asset is intriguing and worthy of further evaluation.  The proof will be in whether the dose can be sufficiently elevated in patients to deliver a viable clinical response in selected patients.
