Cumulative Review of LSB-01-004 (Autigen)
CMC: Reviewer’s score of 3 is appropriate for this asset. Main technical challenges for delivery have been addressed. Remaining CMC needs are consistent with a preclinical development program.
Regulatory: Reviewer’s score of 3 appropriately reflects the potential for Breakthrough Designation due to lack of effective therapies in the indication.
Clinical: Clinical review was conducted by an expert clinical audiologist who rated the subdomains as 5, 5, and 4, clearly suggesting the feasibility of conducting the clinical studies. However, two elements temper my enthusiasm. First, while there are simple clinical measures, there’s no definitive agreement on the level of effect that represents a clinically meaningful therapeutic response. It’s important here to point out the distinction between a statistically significant outcome and a clinically meaningful outcome. Second, conduct of the trials will be most effective if there is coordination between the primary referring clinic (typically emergency room or urgent care clinic) and the treating specialist audiologist. Therefore, I recommend downgrading the overall Clinical score to 4 reflecting high feasibility with some challenges.
Technical: No external technical review has been conducted at this time. However, preclinical data in the confidential package, commentary from the Clinical Reviewer and research by the current reviewer suggest a most-relevant scores of
Scientific Merit = 4: Promising evidence documenting an association, based on strong genetic association between the target and condition.
Level of Supporting Data = 3: Comprehensive preclinical package complete and demonstrates safety and efficacy. Noting that this refers to preclinical data.
Commercial: The Commercial reviewer’s subdomain scores of 5, 5 and 5 are largely appropriate according to the metrics provided as they reflect the total market potential. However, given the limited subpopulation targeted for the initial approval, the Total Addressable US Market subdomain score should be reduced to 3 (Non-rare disease and ability to treat 10-25% of patients with the indication (<1% of population) based on information the “BI Deep Dive” assessment provided. So, Commercial scores are adjusted to 3, 5 and 5.
Overall Cumulative Assessment: Overall, the Autigen hearing loss asset addresses a major unmet medical need. The extent of its addressable market is questionable, but the path to approval is in an indication with a market sufficient to justify development. Many of the technical hurdles experienced in other programs have been addressed, especially delivery. This asset is well-positioned to yield a clear clinical go/no-go decision within 3-5 years.
