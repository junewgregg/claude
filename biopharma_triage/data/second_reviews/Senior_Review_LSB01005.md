Detailed review of LSB-01-005 (CDK7​)
CMC: I support the CMC scoring given the early lead-optimization stage with foundational developability data, but no designated development candidate. The program is assigned to a numerical value of 2 until additional CMC data can be provided
Regulatory: I agree with the scoring. The indication appears to be small cell lung cancer which makes it qualify for Orphan designation (several other products have received the designation for this disease). Thus, a CDK7 inhibitor for SCLC could qualify for Breakthrough therapy as the condition is serious but it depends on the early clinical data
Clinical: I agree with the clinical assessment.  For this class of assets which have been studied extensively, the clinical results have been unimpressive, and no specific developmental direction has emerged for the class.  Several highly selective CDK7 inhibitors, such LY-3405105, SY-1365 were discontinued due to insufficient activity and significant toxicity.
Technical: The technical review is appropriate for the MOA rationale and the level of evidence supporting this asset. There is precedent data from emerging/early assets in the field that showed stable disease and, in some cases, partial response.  However, there is no clinical data for CDK7 degrader.  Level of evidence is supported by other CDKi assets for which literature is provided, e.g., YKL-5–124 and YKL-5–167.
Commercial: I agree with the commercial review related to “addressable market, “competition” and potential for “premium pricing”.  There is a lack of clarity on what the target opportunity for the primary indication might be which confounds accuracy in score assignment.
Collective Review by PWT: LSB-01-005 is at a relatively early stage of development with a lead candidate yet to be identified.  For assets with similar targets/MOAs the overall benefit to risk profiles have been marginal.
