Detailed review of LSB-01-003 (NVS-001)
CMC:  I can follow the arguments for and agree with the scoring.
Regulatory:  I can agree with the scoring.
Clinical: I can agree with the scoring.
Technical: Not based on asset data, but from competitor drugs, scores are high (4-5)
Commercial: I think the score for the total addressable market is too high. My question is what market remains for injectables after oral drugs come on the market. Also, I believe pricing will become a very important issue. Decrease scoring to 4
Overall, second opinion review:
Although risk for clinical failure is low, there are too many late pre-clinical and early clinical assets in development at well financed ($100+) start-up companies ahead of this asset and competing for the same pharma buyers. Also, major concerns about differentiation from (almost approved) direct competitor drug (Amgen’s Maridebart Cafraglutide) and injectable versus oral drug in the space makes me question transactability in 3-4 years. Recommend deprioritizing this asset.
