# Second Review — LSB-02-008 · S095042 OX40 mAb (Servier)
**Panel Determination: Deprioritize**
**Analyst Rating: Deprioritize (consensus)**

## Asset Overview
S095042 is a subcutaneous OX40 monoclonal antibody from Servier targeting chronic spontaneous urticaria (CSU). The asset is in IND-enabling stage. CSU is the proposed lead indication.

## Why It Was Deprioritized

**Landscape is crowded and the window has closed.** Multiple OX40 and OX40L mAbs are in late-stage development (Sanofi's amlitelimab met its CSU primary endpoint). Dupilumab (Sanofi/Regeneron) is approved in CSU and will face biosimilar competition from 2029/2031 — reducing the commercial upside of a new entrant arriving in 2029+.

**Timeline is unacceptable.** Estimated development path (healthy volunteer Phase 1 followed by Phase 2a) puts first efficacy data in ~2029 with a budget estimate of $50M+. That exceeds LSB's ≤$25M development cost gate and is beyond the ≤3-5 year time-to-value gate.

**Thesis gate failures:** Both the development cost gate ($50M estimated) and the time-to-value gate (2029 readout) are "Not met." An asset that fails two hard gates is Deprioritize regardless of biological rationale.

## Calibration Note
OX40 as a target is not universally negative — but this specific asset, at this stage, in this indication, against this competitive backdrop, fails on commercial and timeline grounds. The mechanism might score differently in a less crowded indication with a faster development path.
