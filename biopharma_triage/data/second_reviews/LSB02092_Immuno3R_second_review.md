# Second Review — LSB-02-092 · Immuno3R / O3R-5671 (Onco3R / ex-Galapagos)
**Panel Determination: Hold**
**Analyst Rating: Diligence → Panel Hold (not Deprioritize)**

## Asset Overview
O3R-5671 is an oral, selective SIK3 inhibitor developed by Onco3R, originally from Galapagos. It is in Phase 1A with initial safety data. The lead indication focus at time of review was psoriasis, with UC data from Phase 1A and a psoriasis Phase 1b study planned imminently.

## Why It Landed at Hold (Not Deprioritize)

**SIK pathway has biological rationale.** SIK3 inhibition suppresses pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF) via a mechanism distinct from JAK inhibitors, offering potential selectivity advantages. The psoriasis indication, if the planned 1b reads out positively, would substantially de-risk the asset.

**Hold — not Deprioritize — because the psoriasis data will be informative.** The panel recognized that a negative or positive psoriasis 1b would shift the rating meaningfully. Recommending Hold is a deliberate "wait and see" posture, not a pass.

**Phase 1A tox is relatively clean so far.** Initial safety data showed TNF suppression without the off-target signals seen with less selective SIK inhibitors.

## Why It Did Not Reach Prioritize

**Preclinical evidence package for psoriasis is incomplete.** No psoriasis animal model data were available at time of review. The lead indication (psoriasis) had not been validated preclinically, making the mechanistic extrapolation from UC data a stretch.

**Galapagos provenance raises questions.** Why would Galapagos out-license a lead SIK3 program? Their track record in small-molecule IM is mixed. The inference that prior VC/pharma engagement had not achieved traction is a caution signal.

**Bone/metabolic tox liabilities not resolved.** SIK3 biology involves bone formation and glucose/lipid homeostasis. Long-term tox in chronic psoriasis setting (years of dosing) has not been characterized.

**Presentation quality was poor.** Multiple reviewers noted a disorganized, incomplete deck — a signal about sponsor quality and readiness for partnership.

## Panel Swing Factor
The upcoming psoriasis Phase 1b is the decisive gating event. Panel chose Hold specifically to revisit after those data are available. A positive psoriasis read would move this to Prioritize; a negative or unclear read would move to Deprioritize.

## Reviewer Consensus
Three reviewers. Reviewer 1: scientifically interested but concerned about tox (bone, metabolic) and Galapagos track record. Reviewer 2: found the presentation disorganized; appreciated the TNF signal and Phase 1A clean tox; explicitly recommended waiting for the psoriasis 1b. Reviewer 3 provided a tiebreaker consistent with Hold.

## Calibration Note
Hold ≠ Deprioritize. The panel uses Hold when a single gating experiment or event would materially change the rating. Hold assets should be calendared for re-review when the expected data readout occurs. Do not conflate Hold with "low conviction Diligence."
