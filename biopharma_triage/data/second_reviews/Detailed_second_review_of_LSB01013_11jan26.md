Detailed review of LSB-01-013 (SYB1.25)
CMC:  I strongly believe score is too high , from the information provided it seems to me this asset is not ready to go for IND enabling CMC development. Questions I have are:
Why 2 X ScFv and not conventional bi-specific mAb?
Is the asset humanized
What mutations in the Fc tail have been used to avoid mispairing? What is the level of mispairing?
What expression system was used so far and what expression levels and final product yield?
Was the sequence analyzed for potential immunogenicity and pharmaceutical liabilities?
Regulatory:  This needs to be redone as the reviewer did not understand what the drug modality is.
Clinical: As the reviewer indicated, it is unclear how the IP stakeholder is positioning this asset. My believe is that it should be looked at as an improved ICI drug which is more potent that just blocking PD-L1/PD1 (by neutralizing IL25) and has potentially also less side effects (irAEs) due to neutralizing IL25.
Technical: I agree with the reviewer that the information provided does not allow a proper scoring. I do however think that (and we need to dig into that if we want) there is compelling preclinical data (but I would need to understand the models used and investigate if these models can test the drugs MoA).
Commercial: I like to discuss if the asset was evaluated on the right clinical indication (see also comments on clinical. I would question if this drug can be positioned to treat ICI related irAEs. This is an ICI drug itself, with the potential benefit of improved potency and less side effects. Certainly, premium pricing, but a very competitive space. How will the drug compare to the current PD1xVGEF bispecifics?
Overal second opinion review: Project is too early to meet current LevelSetBio inclusion criteria and there are too many unanswered questions to do a proper rating. Propose to de-prioritize.
